REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) No. 2026-IT-114
Issuing Agency: City of Lakeview Public Works Department

PROJECT TITLE: Modernization of the Citizen Service Request Portal

1. BACKGROUND
The City of Lakeview currently operates a citizen service request system (pothole reports,
sanitation issues, code enforcement, etc.) built on a 15-year-old legacy platform. The system
is difficult to maintain, lacks mobile support, and cannot integrate with the City's GIS or
work-order systems. The City seeks a qualified vendor to design, build, and deploy a modern,
cloud-based replacement.

2. SCOPE OF WORK
The selected vendor shall:
  a. Design and develop a cloud-hosted citizen service request portal accessible via web and
     mobile browser, supporting at least 50,000 monthly active residents.
  b. Integrate the portal with the City's existing GIS platform (Esri ArcGIS) for
     location-based reporting and routing.
  c. Integrate with the City's work-order management system for automatic ticket creation
     and status sync.
  d. Provide a public-facing dashboard showing aggregate request status and resolution times.
  e. Ensure the system meets WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
  f. Provide role-based access for City staff (dispatchers, field crews, supervisors,
     administrators).
  g. Migrate historical request data (approx. 380,000 records) from the legacy system.
  h. Provide a security architecture consistent with NIST 800-53 moderate-impact controls.
  i. Provide 12 months of post-launch support and a knowledge transfer plan to City IT staff.
  j. Deliver the system within 9 months of contract award.

3. PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS
Proposals must include, at minimum:
  - Executive Summary
  - Understanding of the Problem
  - Technical Approach and Methodology
  - Proposed Project Timeline / Schedule
  - Team Qualifications and Key Personnel
  - Relevant Past Performance (minimum 3 examples)
  - Compliance Matrix addressing each requirement in Section 2
  - Cost Proposal (separate sealed document, not addressed here)

4. EVALUATION CRITERIA
  - Technical Approach: 35%
  - Team Qualifications & Past Performance: 25%
  - Accessibility & Security Approach: 20%
  - Schedule Feasibility: 10%
  - Cost (evaluated separately): 10%

5. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
Proposals are due no later than 5:00 PM local time, 45 days from the date of RFP issuance.
